# Clinical validation limitations

This repository is a **technical** prototype evaluated on synthetic cases and a synthetic pharmacy network.

It has **not** been validated against real prescriptions, real pharmacists, or real patients. Therefore the following conclusions are **out of scope**:

- AI outperforms pharmacists.
- AI may autonomously prescribe or dispense.
- The system reduced real adverse drug reactions.
- Synthetic fill-rate or recall numbers represent clinical effectiveness.
- The product is ready for production pharmacy deployment.

A future real-world protocol would need ethics approval, prospective pharmacist adjudication, site-specific knowledge governance, and independent clinical endpoints. None of that is claimed here.
